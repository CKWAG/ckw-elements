<div className="docs-section">
  <div className="docs-section-title">Naming</div>
  <div className="docs-description">
    Names are kebab-case: <code>noun</code>, optionally followed by a modifier, a direction,
    <code>-circle</code> and <code>-filled</code> (always last). Outline icons have no suffix, for
    example
    <code>arrow-left-circle</code> and <code>arrow-left-circle-filled</code>. The component name is
    the PascalCase form with an <code>Icon</code> prefix: <code>IconArrowLeftCircle</code>.
  </div>
</div>

<div className="docs-section">
  <div className="docs-section-title">Standard</div>
  <div className="docs-description">
    24px canvas, glyph at most 20px with a 2px safe area, 1.67px stroke with round caps and joins,
    one flat vector in one colour (<code>currentColor</code>). Prefer named imports such as{' '}
    <code>IconSearch</code> in apps: the by-name <code>Icon</code> component references every icon,
    so bundlers cannot drop unused ones.
  </div>
</div>
